# App Store Preview Video Script: "Ditch the Equals Sign"

**Target Length:** 30 seconds
**Vibe:** Fast-paced, TikTok style, high-energy electronic beat. Dark mode aesthetics.
**Target Audience:** High school/college students tired of slow calculators.

## Shot List & Script

### Shot 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:05)
*   **Visual:** Split screen. Left side: A dusty, clunky 1990s TI-84 with a hand painfully tapping `(`, `(`, `(`, `4`, `+`, `3`, `)`, `/`, `...`. Right side: The sleek, neon, dark-mode StackCalc32 interface.
*   **Audio (Voiceover):** "Why are you still pressing equals?"
*   **Text on Screen (Bold, Neon Green):** DITCH THE EQUALS SIGN.

### Shot 2: The Action (0:05 - 0:15)
*   **Visual:** Full screen of StackCalc32. A thumb is lightning-fast tapping numbers: `4`, `ENT`, `3`, `+`, `5`, `*`. The stack beautifully animates, popping numbers up and pushing results down. The haptic feedback rings visually. 
*   **Audio (Voiceover):** "StackCalc32 uses Stack Math. It's the ultimate mental cheat code for your math homework."
*   **Text on Screen (Pop-ups matching action):** NO PARENTHESES. RAW SPEED.

### Shot 3: Plotting (0:15 - 0:22)
*   **Visual:** The user swipes right. A complex polynomial equation evaluates and instantly morphs into a full-screen, vibrant, neon interactive graph. The user pinches to zoom smoothly.
*   **Audio (Voiceover):** "Instantly plot any equation in full screen."
*   **Text on Screen:** DYNAMIC PLOTTING.

### Shot 4: Hardware & Watch (0:22 - 0:26)
*   **Visual:** Fast cuts. 1) The physical StackCalc32 hardware sitting on a desk. 2) The Apple Watch version on a wrist calculating a tip.
*   **Audio (Voiceover):** "On your phone, your watch, or on your desk."
*   **Text on Screen:** EXAM-SAFE COMPANION.

### Shot 5: Outro (0:26 - 0:30)
*   **Visual:** The StackCalc32 logo slams onto a pure black screen. The App Store "Download" badge appears.
*   **Audio (Voiceover):** "Download StackCalc32 and never get lost in a formula again."
*   **Text on Screen:** THE ULTIMATE MATH CHEAT CODE.
