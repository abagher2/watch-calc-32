Description
Not your Daddy's calculator. WatchCalc 32 is an opinionated, modern take on the legendary RPN scientific calculators, purpose-built for today’s students, schools, and exam environments.

We’ve captured the authentic feel of vintage engineering calculators while intentionally stripping out programmable features. The result? A distraction-free, exam-safe tool that gives you pure mathematical power across your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

### Key Features

• Authentic RPN Logic: Experience the speed and efficiency of traditional stack-based RPN entry without the clutter of modern bloated apps.
• Purpose-Built for Schools: Intentionally non-programmable. WatchCalc 32 is designed to be the ultimate exam-safe tool, keeping the focus strictly on solving problems.
• Extensive Mathematical Engine: Tackle advanced operations including exact fractions, complex numbers, statistics, trigonometry, and base conversions (HEX, OCT, BIN, DEC).
• Advanced Equation Solver & Plotting: Evaluate complex equations and instantly visualize them with dynamic, full-screen interactive graphing tools.
• True Cross-Platform Design: Fully optimized for all your devices. Enjoy a hyper-efficient layout on Apple Watch, one-handed portrait mode on iPhone, and an expansive Voyager-style landscape mode on iPad.
• Premium Vintage Feel: Tactile haptic feedback and incredibly detailed UI elements that make you feel like you're holding a premium piece of hardware.
